Home loans and mortgages

Residential mortgage financing is extended subject to underwriting approval and verification of the information provided in the application. An independent appraisal must also establish that the property has sufficient value to support the requested loan amount.

Eligibility and qualification requirements

Applicants seeking conventional financing are generally required to demonstrate a debt-to-income ratio that does not exceed the maximum permitted under applicable investor guidelines. This calculation incorporates all recurring monthly obligations, including installment loans, revolving accounts, and anticipated housing expenses such as principal, interest, taxes, and insurance.

Private mortgage insurance considerations

Where the loan-to-value ratio at origination exceeds eighty percent of the appraised value, the borrower will ordinarily be obligated to maintain private mortgage insurance. Cancellation may be requested once the outstanding principal balance has been sufficiently reduced, in accordance with the procedures described in the applicable servicing disclosures.

Adjustable rate provisions

Adjustable rate loans are characterized by an initial fixed-rate period, after which the interest rate is recalculated periodically by reference to a published index plus a specified margin. Individual adjustments and the cumulative adjustment over the term of the loan remain constrained by the periodic and lifetime limitations set out in the promissory note.

Prospective borrowers are encouraged to review the complete disclosures before submitting an application, as the availability of particular programs is contingent upon occupancy, property type, and prevailing market conditions.
