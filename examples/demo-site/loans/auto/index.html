Auto loans

We lend for new cars and used cars. Your rate is fixed, so your payment stays the same each month. There is no fee to apply.

Get pre-approved today

Get your loan lined up before you shop. A pre-approval tells you how much you can spend. It also helps you say no to dealer add-ons. To see what you may pay, click here.

What you need to apply

  • A photo ID
  • Proof of income, such as a pay stub
  • The price of the car, if you have picked one

How long it takes

Most members get an answer in one business day. If we need more from you, we will call. We do not charge a fee if you pay the loan off early.
