Savings accounts

A savings account is a safe place to keep money you do not need today. We pay you interest on what you save. You can start with five dollars.

Share savings

Every member has a share savings account. Your first five dollars is your share in the credit union. It makes you an owner. You earn interest on every dollar after that.

Holiday and goal accounts

Saving for a trip or for the holidays? Open a goal account and give it a name. Set up a small transfer each payday. Small amounts add up over a year.

How your money is kept safe

Your savings are insured up to the legal limit. If you have questions about coverage, call us. We will walk you through it.
