Banking that belongs to its members

We are a credit union. That means our members own us. We do not answer to outside investors. Each member gets one vote, and each vote counts the same.

What you can do here

You can open a free checking account in about ten minutes. You can start a savings account with five dollars. You can also apply for an auto loan or a home loan online.

Who can join

You can join if you live, work, or go to school in the county. Your family can join too. You need a photo ID and five dollars to open your share account. That first deposit makes you an owner.

New to buying a home?

Buying your first home can feel like a lot. Start with our first-time home buyer guide. It walks you through each step in plain words. Then check our current rates to see what a loan may cost you each month.

Need help?

Call us or stop by a branch. A real person will answer. You can learn more about our credit union and how we got our start.
