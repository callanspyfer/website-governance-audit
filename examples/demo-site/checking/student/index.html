Student checking

Student checking is for members ages 13 to 22. It has no monthly fee. You get a free debit card and a mobile app that shows where your money goes.

Built for a first account

A first account should be simple. This one has no overdraft fees. If you do not have the money, the card will not go through. That keeps a small mistake from turning into a big bill.

What parents should know

Members under 18 need a parent or guardian on the account. The adult can see the balance and set a daily spending limit. At age 18, the member can take over the account alone.

How to open one

Visit a branch with the student and one adult. Bring a photo ID for the adult. Bring a school ID or birth record for the student. You will need five dollars for the first deposit.
