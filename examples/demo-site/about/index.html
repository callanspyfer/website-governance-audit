About our credit union

We opened in 1954 in the back room of a hardware store. Twelve mill workers each put in five dollars. They wanted a fair place to save and to borrow. That is still the whole idea.

How a credit union works

A bank is owned by its investors. A credit union is owned by its members. When we do well, we do not pay outside owners. We give it back to you with lower loan rates and fewer fees.

Who runs it

Members elect the board. Board members are not paid. They are your neighbors. They meet each month, and you are welcome to attend the yearly meeting and vote.

Where we work

We serve people who live, work, or go to school in the county. We have three branches and a call center staffed by local people.
