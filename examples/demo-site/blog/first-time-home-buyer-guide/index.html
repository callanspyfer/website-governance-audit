First-time home buyer guide

Buying a home has four main steps. You find out what you can spend. You save for the up-front costs. You get a loan. Then you close. This guide covers each one.

Step 1: Find out what you can spend

Add up what you earn each month before taxes. Then add up your monthly debts, like a car loan or a card. Most lenders want your debts plus your new house payment to stay under about four tenths of your pay.

Step 2: Save for up-front costs

You will need a down payment. You will also need money for closing costs, which are the fees to finish the sale. Ask your lender for a list of these fees early so there are no surprises.

Step 3: Get a loan

Ask for a pre-approval before you shop. It shows sellers you are serious. Our home loan team can help. For a look back at how rates have moved, read our 2019 rate update.

Step 4: Close

At closing you sign the loan papers and pay your up-front costs. Then you get the keys. Read each page before you sign. Ask about any fee you do not know.
